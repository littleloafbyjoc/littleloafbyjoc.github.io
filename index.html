about little loaf by joc

hi everyone! i'm jocelyn, and i'm so excited to share a little piece of something i love with you. what started as a simple love for baking has grown into something i can share with others, one loaf at a time.

each loaf is homemade, slowly fermented, and freshly baked to order with lots of love and care. whether you're supporting my little baking journey or simply stopping by for some good bread, i'm so happy you're here!

thank you so much for supporting my little home bakery. every order means the world to me.
